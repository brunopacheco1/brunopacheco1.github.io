This site does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites. It keeps no profile of you
and runs no ads.

## What is measured

To know which posts are read and whether the pages load quickly, the site uses
[Umami Cloud](https://umami.is), a cookieless analytics service with its data stored in the European
Union. For each visit, Umami records:

- the page, the site you came from (the referrer) and the time of the visit;
- your browser, operating system, device type, screen size and language;
- the country, region and city, worked out from your IP address. According to Umami, the IP address
  itself is not stored.

The site also measures how fast each page loads and responds (the
[Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals): LCP, INP, CLS, FCP and TTFB), so slow pages can be
fixed. These are timings of the page, not information about you.

Umami counts unique visitors without cookies, so the numbers cannot be linked to a person. The
statistics are only used in aggregate, to improve the site.

If your browser sends **Do Not Track**, nothing is measured. Blocking the Umami script with a content
blocker works too, and the site keeps working without it.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com). Like any web server, GitHub receives
your IP address when you open a page, and logs it for security purposes. See the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
